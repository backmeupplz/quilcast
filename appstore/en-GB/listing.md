## Description (max 4000)
Quilcast is a small, open-source client for Farcaster, the decentralised social network that no company owns. It talks straight to the Hypersnap network, signs everything you post on your own device, and keeps your keys there too.

Farcaster is web3 social media: your account is registered on chain, so you can take it to any client, Quilcast included.

Read your following feed and what's trending, open threads with their whole context, and look people up by name or search the text of casts. Post, reply, quote, like, recast and follow, attach photos and videos, and post into channels. Notifications group your likes, recasts and follows, and show replies, mentions and quotes as they arrive.

Direct messages and live audio Spaces from farcaster.xyz work here too: chat one to one or in groups, listen to what's live, raise your hand, or host your own room.

Farcaster mini apps open right inside Quilcast, with a Discover list of the most popular ones. They can sign you in with your Farcaster account and use the built-in crypto wallet, which holds as many Ethereum wallets as you like, shows balances, tokens and NFTs across Base, Ethereum, Optimism, Arbitrum and Polygon, and lets you send tokens or swap them between those networks. Every signature and transaction a mini app asks for is shown to you before it is signed.

Personalise it with MySpace-style profile looks (backgrounds, fonts, a profile song, a Top 8, stickers and moving effects), a Winamp on your profile that plays your YouTube videos and playlists for visitors, and whole-app themes, including every theme from Quorum's gallery, so the colours, fonts and backgrounds are yours to choose. Casts in other languages can be translated with a tap.

Create a new Farcaster account in the app, or sign in with the recovery phrase you already use elsewhere. Mute, block or report anyone from the ⋯ menu on their casts and profile.

Quilcast is free, has no adverts and no tracking, and its code is MIT-licensed at https://git.borodutch.com/backmeupplz/quilcast.
