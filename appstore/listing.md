## App Review notes
Quilcast is a client for Farcaster, an open social network. Feeds, profiles, search, Discover mini apps and themes all work without an account. Creating a Farcaster account needs a small Optimism ETH payment for the on-chain registration, so please review the signed-out experience. Mute, block and report are in the ⋯ menu on every cast and profile; reports go to support@borodutch.com and are acted on within 24 hours (taken-down casts and accounts are hidden for everyone). People accept the Terms of Use on first launch.
