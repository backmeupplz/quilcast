Remind me

How long should I wait before bringing this cast back?

The reminder comes as a notification from Remind me, so it asks to turn those on the first time.

Remind me brings a cast back later as a notification. To set one, open it from a cast’s ⋯ menu.

One-click reminders

Put a reminder straight into the ⋯ menu under every cast: one tap sets it and a toast says so, with no window to open.

“Ask how long” asks for the time in the menu itself. To take one off, open Cast actions in Quilcast (the last line of any cast’s ⋯ menu).
