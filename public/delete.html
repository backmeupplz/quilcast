Delete your Quilcast data

Quilcast, by Borodutch Labs Inc.

Quilcast has no accounts of its own: you use a Farcaster account, which lives on the public Farcaster network and the Optimism blockchain, not with us. Here is how to remove what Quilcast keeps.

In the app: Delete account

In Quilcast, open Settings, choose Delete account and confirm. Quilcast deletes everything our server keeps for that account at once: your profile look, mute and block lists, drafts, and the photos and videos you uploaded to our server. It then signs out, removing the account's keys and the signing key Quilcast added for this device (the network is told to stop accepting it). Uninstalling the app removes everything else it stored on the device. Sign out, also in Settings, removes the keys without touching our server.

By email

If you can't open the app any more, email support@borodutch.com with your Farcaster username or ID, and within 30 days we delete the same data, plus saved themes and photos uploaded before October 31, 2026. You can also ask for only some of it to be deleted.

On Farcaster

Casts, likes and follows are public messages on the Farcaster network, which nobody, us included, can erase for everyone. You can delete your own casts from the ⋯ menu, which tells the network to drop them. Your Farcaster ID stays registered on the blockchain.
