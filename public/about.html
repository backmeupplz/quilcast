About Quilcast

A Farcaster client for Hypersnap, built by Borodutch Labs.

Quilcast is an open-source Farcaster client you run straight in your browser at quilcast.com (or via the iPhone and Android apps), talking directly to Hypersnap, Quilibrium's Farcaster network. Reads hit a public Hypersnap node and every cast is signed locally in your browser, so your keys stay in browser storage with no Quilcast server sitting in the middle. There are no Quilcast accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only server-side piece is optional X crossposting, which stays off until you enable it and uses a separate cast-only key kept on our server. (Checked November 9, 2026.)

Signing in

Sign in with your existing Farcaster recovery phrase (same setup as Warpcast and Quorum) or connect a custody wallet like MetaMask or Rabby. If you're starting from scratch, Quilcast generates a recovery phrase in your browser; send roughly $0.20 of ETH on Optimism to it, and it registers the account, adds a signing key, and claims your username.

What it does

You get following and trending feeds, threads, profiles, notifications, and search, along with casts, replies, quotes, likes, recasts, follows, and channel posts. You can attach up to four photos, videos or GIFs per cast (a quoted cast takes one of the four). Live Farcaster direct messages are built in with group chats, message requests, and muting, and you can listen to, raise a hand in, or host Farcaster Spaces audio rooms.

Quilcast also ships a built-in wallet for Base, Ethereum, Optimism, Arbitrum, and Polygon with balances, tokens, NFTs, and sends, plus in-app Farcaster and Base mini apps with notifications. Casts in other languages translate inline, and private mutes and blocks sync across your devices. Our own Laya spam model scores posts and lets you pick the threshold for labeling or hiding spam; the raw scores are public and free at spam.quilcast.com.

On the lighter side, you can deck out profiles with MySpace-style layouts, swap app themes, drop a classic-skin Winamp player onto your profile to play YouTube playlists, or flip on Quorum mode to mirror Quilibrium's Quorum layout.
